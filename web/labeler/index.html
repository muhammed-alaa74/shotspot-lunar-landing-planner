How to label

  1. Pick an area. Work cell by cell (the 500 m grid).
  2. In a cell, circle every crater with a diameter of 10 m or more (10 pixels). Drag from the center to the rim.
  3. Switch lighting (1-5) when a crater is half in shadow. All lightings show the same place.
  4. Not sure it is a crater? Draw it, then press U (unsure).
  5. When nothing is left in the cell, press D. Only done cells are used for training.
  6. Click Export labels often and send the file. Labels are also kept in this browser.
